## Purpose
Develop practical marketing skills through research, implementation, measurement and reflection. This is a learning portfolio, not a claim that the planned projects have been completed.

Primary practice project: NeoSonik Studios, a proposed rehearsal studio in Sheffield. The business is pre-launch and seeking premises. Do not imply that a studio is open, publish an unconfirmed address, or invent prices, testimonials, bookings or revenue.

## Working routine
Suggested commitment: three 45-minute weekday sessions and one 60-minute review each week. Adjust around existing commitments.
1. Learn a concept from a primary source.
2. Apply it to a defined business problem.
3. Submit work with sources and assumptions.
4. Review feedback and revise.
5. Record what changed and what to test next.

Completion requires a usable output and review, not simply watching a lesson. Certificates are optional supporting evidence.

## Assignment 1: Understand the customer before choosing a channel
Time: approximately 60-90 minutes. Status: not submitted.

### Part A: Complete a one-page business brief
1. Business and current stage.
2. One primary customer group.
3. The problem in the customer's own words. Include the source or label as a hypothesis.
4. Existing alternatives customers use.
5. A proposed offer. Label unconfirmed facilities/prices.
6. One action customers can take now.
7. One measurable objective for the next four weeks. Set the target after checking the baseline.
8. What is known, what is assumed, and what evidence would change the plan.

### Part B: Prepare five customer interview questions
Ask about actual past behaviour rather than hypothetical enthusiasm.
1. Tell me about the last time you booked a rehearsal space.
2. How did you find it and why did you choose it?
3. What did it cost, and what was included?
4. What was frustrating or missing?
5. What would make you change rehearsal space?

Do not collect unnecessary personal data. Do not publish interview identities or private messages.

### Part C: Compare three alternatives
Columns: business/source URL, intended audience, services stated, publicly listed price (or unknown), booking action, evidence date, opportunity to investigate.
Record observed facts separately from your interpretation. No fabricated competitor details.

### Part D: Write one positioning sentence
I help [specific audience] who struggle with [specific problem] to [desired outcome].
For a pre-launch business, identify this as proposed positioning.

### Review rubric
Score each area from 0 to 4:
- Customer specificity
- Quality of evidence
- Clarity of offer and next action
- Link between objective and measurement
- Reflection and next experiment

0 = absent; 1 = vague; 2 = usable with gaps; 3 = clear and supported; 4 = supported, actionable and explicit about limitations.
Revise scores below 3. No score has been awarded yet.
